Hi, I'm Ming-Lung Tsai — you can also call me Stanley. My curiosity runs across a lot of fields: low-level systems, product building, the realities of society, and the classical humanities. Exploring widely is just my nature, but I keep one thing in check — breadth is no excuse to give up depth. What I want is to connect these fields and make something only I can make.

Right now I'm studying Computer Science at National Taiwan University, and I'm finding my way into Confidential Computing at SSLab. My work touches Arm TrustZone and Google pKVM — the topic doesn't even have a formal title yet. In short: using the Android Virtualization Framework (AVF) to virtualize TrustZone. Honestly I'm still in the digging-in phase, but that's exactly what pulls me in — figuring out how the lowest, hardest layer of a system actually works.

I'd rather build something real and throw it at reality to see if it works than sit on an idea. In 2025–2026 I was part of the 18th cohort of NTU's Creativity and Entrepreneurship Program (CEP), where we built **CouPro**, a localized, two-sided deals platform centered on campus life — think of it as a general-purpose version of McDonald's stamp card. CouPro placed third in the program, and we also received an offer for Silicon Valley participation through the [2026 NTU AI Builders Challenge](https://cep.ntu.edu.tw/ai-創業矽谷種子競賽/). After launching it and running it for a year, we're now thinking about what to take on next. That loop of "build it first, then let reality give you the answer," and the Market-First mindset behind it, was the most valuable thing I took from this year.

Beyond tech and startups, I've long cared about social issues. I served as vice president of NTU's Homeless Service Club for its 5th and 6th terms, doing a fair amount of organizing and on-campus curation. During that time we built an empowerment channel with NGOs so students could become volunteers in advocacy and service, and we published the club's first journal, which opened up new revenue and outreach at local markets and similar venues. The same thing drives me throughout: being curious about issues and about people, then actually going in and making contact — not just watching from the outside, or stopping at a symbolic understanding.

Outside of all this, I spend a good amount of time reading in the humanities. Lately I've been learning Sanskrit, getting ready to study ancient India and Vedic culture down the road; and I read a bit of philosophy now and then, simply because I enjoy it.

As for this blog: besides everyday thoughts and slices of life, I'll write up the insights and the pitfalls from projects I've worked on, and even share the ones I'm building right now. My hope is that reading it will feel a little like reading a novel — and that this About page keeps growing as I do.

If you're into low-level systems, deep-tech startups, or the place where technology meets society — whether you want to talk research, talk startups, or just trade ideas about the world — I'd love to hear from you.

## 中文

Hi，我是蔡明龍（Ming-Lung Tsai），你也可以叫我 Stanley！我的好奇心橫跨很多領域——系統底層、產品開發、社會現場、古典人文。探索各式各樣的領域是我的天性，但我也時時警惕自己：廣度不該成為放棄深度的理由。我期許自己能把這些領域的知識結合起來，做出只有我能做到的事。

目前我就讀台大資工系，正在 SSLab 裡摸索 Confidential Computing 這個方向，手上碰的是與 Arm TrustZone、Google pKVM 相關的題目（連正式的 title 都還沒定下來）。簡單說，就是想用 AVF（Android Virtualization Framework）把 TrustZone 虛擬化。老實說我還在往裡鑽的階段，但真正吸引我的正是這件事：搞懂一個系統最底層、最難的那一塊，到底是怎麼運作的。

比起停留在想法，我更喜歡真的把產品做出來，丟進現實裡看它會不會 work。2025–2026 年我是台大創創學程（CEP）第 18 屆的成員，我們做的項目是 CouPro，一個以校園為中心打造的在地化優惠雙邊平台，可以想像成通用版的麥當勞甜心卡。CouPro 拿到學程第三名，我們也透過 [2026 NTU AI Builders Challenge 矽谷創業種子甄選競賽](https://cep.ntu.edu.tw/ai-創業矽谷種子競賽/)取得赴矽谷交流的 offer。在項目啟動、實際運營一年之後，我們目前正在思考下一個題目的方向。這種「先做出來、再讓現實給答案」的過程，還有背後 Market First 的思維，是我這一年最有收穫的地方。

技術和創業之外，我也長期關注社會議題。我是台大無家者服務社第五、六屆的副社長，做過不少組織串聯與校園策展的工作。任內，我們和 NGO 建立起培力的交流管道，讓校內學生有機會成為議題倡議與服務的志工；也發行了社團的第一本社刊，為社團在地方市集等場合開拓了新的收入與行銷管道。驅動我的始終是同一件事：對議題好奇，也對人好奇，然後真的走進去接觸，而不只是旁觀、或停留在符號層次的理解。

除了這些，平常我也花不少時間讀人文的書。最近在學梵文，為之後研究古印度與吠陀文化做準備；偶爾也讀點哲學，單純因為喜歡。

我的blog除了對於生活的碎念跟分享之外，還會整理自己過去做過的項目上，得到的Insights或踩過的坑，甚至分享自己正在做的項目。希望未來大家看我的Blog會覺得很像在看小說；並且About頁面會也隨著我的累積而動態成長。

如果你也對系統底層、deep tech 創業，或科技與社會的交界有興趣——想聊研究、聊 startup，或只是交換一些對世界的想法——都很歡迎來找我。
