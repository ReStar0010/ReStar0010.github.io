Today I sat on the floor of my room, staring at the ceiling half-reflected in
the glass. The moment, and my experience of it, felt unreal. Saying that
something is unreal should mean I know what real is. But what I call real may
just be a feeling with less noise and less blur. All right, enough of that. This
post is a simple record of what I plan to publish on this blog.

The blog will mainly hold accounts of projects I am working on now or have
worked on before, with occasional notes and observations about life. Lately I
have felt that looking back at what I have done matters. Turning it into a story
I can continue building is another matter. In the past, I relied on my memory to
imagine and recall. Predictably, I ended up with poorly supported conclusions.
For important experiences in my life, I had only vague impressions and
fragmented images. For the former, I need a medium in which to unfold my
thinking. There is nothing wrong with the latter, but I want another way to
commemorate the memories of the past.

So the reason I write a blog is simple: I use language and symbols to help
myself live a more reflective life.
